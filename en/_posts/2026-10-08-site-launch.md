Welcome to the website of **Corvus Mind**, a French-speaking open source intelligence team. This launch is a chance to tell you who we are, how we work, and what you will find here.

## Who we are

Corvus Mind is an OSINT (*Open Source INTelligence*) team that trains and competes in 100% open source CTFs. Behind the crow lies a simple conviction: in a connected world, most of what matters is already public. The whole job is to find it, cross-check it and verify it.

We love investigations that start from a detail (a photo, a reused username, a forgotten archive) and that, through cross-checking, end up delivering a solid answer.

## What we do

Our field covers several complementary disciplines, which a good investigation almost always combines:

* **people search**: linking identifiers and accounts to rebuild a digital footprint;
* **geolocation**: finding where an image was taken from its visual clues;
* **SOCMINT**: reading the traces left on social media;
* **image analysis**: metadata, reverse search, authenticity checks.

## Our first results

Two competitions got us started:

* **DeepThreats 2026** (Campus OSINT - DGA): 25th out of 491 teams, in a 100% OSINT CTF.
* **Opération Culot - Bpifrance** (#BIG2026): 3rd out of 223 online, 5th out of 24 on-site.

Details on the competitions and partner organisations are on the [results]({{ '/en/results/' | relative_url }}) page.

## Our code of conduct

One rule is non-negotiable for us: **work only from public and legally accessible sources.** No intrusion, no bypassing of access controls, no impersonation. We observe, cross-check and document, with respect for privacy and the legal framework. If getting a piece of information requires deceiving someone or forcing access, it is no longer OSINT.
