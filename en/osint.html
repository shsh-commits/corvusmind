OPEN SOURCE INTELLIGENCE

OSINT

OSINT (Open Source INTelligence) is the art of collecting, cross-checking and analysing publicly available information to answer a question. No hacking, no intrusion: only what anyone can legally access, but read methodically.

THE PRINCIPLE

Turning public data into intelligence

A single piece of data is worth nothing on its own. OSINT means assembling scattered fragments (a photo, a username, a date, a domain name) until they tell something verifiable. Three rules guide all the work:

Open sources

We only use what is public and legally accessible. No intrusion, no bypassing of access controls.

Cross-checking

A piece of information is only kept once confirmed by at least two independent sources. A single clue is a lead, not a proof.

Traceability

Every conclusion is documented: source, date, screenshot. What isn't sourced doesn't exist.

SPECIALTIES

The disciplines of OSINT

OSINT branches into complementary sub-fields. A serious investigation almost always combines several of them.

SOCMINT

Social media

Analysis of the traces left on platforms: profiles, posts, follows, metadata. A reused username is often the best bridge between two identities.
GEOINT

Geolocation

Determining where an image was taken from visual clues: shop signs, architecture, vegetation, shadows, road signs. Reverse search and mapping do the rest.
IMINT

Image analysis

Reading an image beyond its subject: EXIF metadata, reverse search, manipulation detection, detail extraction. Verify authenticity before using it.
PEOPLE

People search

Linking email, username, phone number and accounts to rebuild a digital footprint, within the legal framework. Pivoting from one identifier to the next.
WEBINT

Web & infrastructure

Mapping a website: subdomains, certificates, DNS, technologies, history. Understanding what is exposed without ever touching it.
CHRONO

Chronolocation

Dating an event or an image: sun position, weather, archives, versions of a page over time. The "when" matters as much as the "where".

METHOD

The intelligence cycle

An investigation is never improvised. It follows a cycle that is repeated until the answer is solid.

  1. 01
    Direction

    Define the question precisely. What are we looking for, why, and what does a valid answer look like?

  2. 02
    Collection

    Gather the relevant open sources: search engines, social networks, archives, public databases. Broad first, then precise.

  3. 03
    Processing

    Clean, decode, organise. Extract metadata, translate, convert, sort the clues.

  4. 04
    Analysis

    Cross-check, test the hypotheses against each other, look for contrary evidence. A lead only becomes a fact once confirmed.

  5. 05
    Dissemination

    Report clearly: conclusion, confidence level, dated sources. The writeup is part of the work.

FRAMEWORK

Ethics & legality

What OSINT is

  • Consulting public, legally accessible sources
  • Observing without interaction or intrusion
  • Rigorous documentation and compliance with the GDPR
  • Serving a legitimate purpose (CTF, research, verification)

What OSINT is not

  • Accessing an account or bypassing a protection
  • Impersonating someone to extract information
  • Harassing, monitoring or targeting a private individual
  • Collecting sensitive data without a legitimate purpose

The line is simple: if getting the information requires deceiving someone, forcing access or violating their privacy, it is no longer OSINT. Corvus Mind sticks to this strictly.
